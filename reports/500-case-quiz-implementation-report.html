500-Case Quiz Expansion

BodyMaps implementation report for engineering handoff

BodyMaps now has a working 500-case development quiz bank instead of one usable case. The bank contains 350 lesion-label-positive and 150 lesion-label-absent cases, works in solo practice and Live Quiz, and includes local CT, masks, QA images, and fast preview volumes. Automated checks passed. Human content review remains intentionally deferred: all 500 regenerated v1.1 packs are pending.

Implementation

What Codex did

  • Pulled source dataDownloaded official PanTS labels and metadata, then staged only the CTs needed for the selected cohort plus reserves.
  • Built reproducible stagingAdded resumable downloads, safe official-archive extraction, mask checks, CT/mask geometry checks, deterministic 350/150 selection, and reserve replacement.
  • Generated quiz packsCreated 500 immutable packs with server-side answers, questions, explanations, viewer cues, provenance, approval state, and QA images.
  • Enabled testing before reviewAdded an explicit development switch that includes pending packs without weakening approved-only production defaults.
  • Wired solo and Live QuizConnected the shared bank to solo attempts, seeded playlists, Live Rooms, private answers, reveal gating, and scoring.
  • Improved load speedGenerated paired low-resolution CT/mask assets and stopped quiz modes from silently downloading full-resolution CT in the background.

Limitations

499 generated packs still need human review. Structural validation checks files, geometry, labels, scoring, and pack shape; it does not certify clinical wording or educational quality.

  • Generation report contains 175 review warnings. These remain visible for later triage.

  • Worktree already contained substantial uncommitted Live Rooms work. It was preserved; no commit or push was made.
